From sketch to heirloom

The making of a jewel.

01
I.

Imagine

Every jewel starts as a conversation and a sketch — proportion, movement and meaning drawn by hand before a single gram of gold is touched.

II.

Cast

Gold is refined, poured and shaped into its first form, carrying the warmth and weight that only well-made metal has.

III.

Set

Stones are chosen one by one and set under magnification by hands that have done this for a lifetime.

IV.

Finish

A final polish, a last inspection, and a signature. Only then is it ready to become part of your story.
